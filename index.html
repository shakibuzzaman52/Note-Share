Google Sheets Backend Setup

Connect your Google Sheet as the zero-database backend.

Deploy the provided script in Google Sheets and paste your Web App URL here:

How it works:

  1. Create a Google Sheet: Add headers in row 1: Department | Section | Date | Course Name | Topic Name | Note Title | Note Link | Status | Submitted At
  2. Deploy Google Apps Script: In your Google Sheet, open Extensions > Apps Script, paste the code from google-apps-script.js, and click Deploy > New deployment > Web app (Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone).
  3. Approve Notes: When students submit notes via this website, rows are automatically inserted with Status: Pending. To publish a note, simply change its status to Approved in the sheet!
