Explore ClassNotes

Find lecture notes, browse study materials, and contribute resources to help your classmates.

Features

  • Department & Section Filter: Find notes specific to your department and section.
  • Course & Topic Filter: Browse notes by course and topic.
  • Interactive Calendar: Find notes by selecting a specific date.
  • Share Notes: Share useful study resources with your classmates.
  • Report Notes: Report notes that are incorrect, broken, or inappropriate.
  • Community Contributions: Upload your own notes and help classmates access useful study materials.
  • Simple Interface: Easily navigate and access shared resources.

How to Find Notes

  1. Select your Department.
  2. Choose your Section.
  3. Select a Course to find relevant notes.
  4. Choose a Topic if needed.
  5. Use the calendar to select a date and view available notes.
  6. Click a note title to open the shared resource.
  7. Use the available Share option to share a note with others.
  8. If you find a broken, incorrect, or inappropriate note, use the Report option.

How to Upload Notes

  1. Click the Add Note button.
  2. Select your Department and Section.
  3. Choose the class date.
  4. Select the relevant Course.
  5. Enter the Topic Name and Note Title.
  6. Add a shareable link to your note, such as a Google Drive link.
  7. Submit the note.

Before submitting:

  • Make sure all information is accurate.
  • Use a clear and descriptive title.
  • Ensure the shared link is accessible to others.
  • Upload only relevant study materials.
Submitted notes may require admin approval before appearing on the website.
