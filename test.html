Problem

You already know which invoices are overdue.

You just haven't had time to do anything about it.

01

It takes too long. Logging in, finding the invoice, writing a reminder, sending it, tracking whether they responded. For one invoice that's fine. For thirty it's a part-time job.

02

It's easy to forget. You meant to follow up on Tuesday. Then a client called. Then it was Friday. That invoice is now 60 days overdue and the conversation just got awkward.

03

Nothing is consistent. Some clients get three reminders. Others slip through completely. There's no system, just good intentions and a messy inbox.
